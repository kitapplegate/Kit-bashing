## Closed questions
- The first Break Room version did not call Jev. Kit believed it did, learned during this interview that it did not, and redirected Grok Bot to wire Jev into the app while the interview continued.
- Kit does not know the original implementation details because Grok Bot did the heavy coding. Do not ask him to reverse-engineer that version again.
- Kit does not remember the exact first set of cards. He remembers that it leaned heavily toward GitHub items and that he marked most of them “MEH.”
- Kit does not have a more specific answer for what unique judgment Jev adds after the upstream agent filters the larger pool. He said it ranks the candidates that survive and receives feedback from his choices.
- The usable wall took about 15 minutes to build. The signal pool refreshes hourly, the wall shows 15 cards, and Jev scores them when Kit opens it.
- Kit supplied Jev pricing as `$0.042 per million input tokens (equivalent to $42 per billion), while output tokens are completely free`; verify this current external fact against TypeSafe before drafting rather than asking him again.
