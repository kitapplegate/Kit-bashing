---

On September 28th, around 8:37 at night, I told Muse, "Blog idea tonight I
was running 3 different AI. Muse, claude and grok bot. Got to be post in
there somewhere."

I was at the dining room table. I turned it into my desk. My laptop sits
on a stand connected to a curved monitor that I had split screen, and my
computer cluster runs to my right with another monitor hooked to it that
shows my daily schedule, what I need to focus on, the weather, and what
tasks I need to complete today. I was wearing shorts and a T-shirt that
says Don't Panic on it, from The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, and I
had a fruit punch or something like that. I was working on a bunch of
different things and I just noticed I had three separate, completely
different AIs going at the same time. Actually it was four, counting Jev.
I've been doing this for maybe three months, and that kind of blew my
mind.

Muse is an AI from Meta. I use it as my personal assistant, it's where I
keep track of the things that are going on.

[Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) is where I do all my
projects, so that's where the coding happens. That night I was in it
working with Brittany, one of my AI agents that runs on
[Hermes](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent), on getting Jev to
categorize and label everything in my [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)
vault so Brittany would be able to find things easier. Jev is an AI from
TypeSafe, and I've [written about building with
it](/Kit-bashing/blog/jev-break-room/) before.

The Grok bot is helping me organize some things for a business I'm
starting, which is going to be the grand reveal I guess. One of my Grok
bots is an inbox bot I named Mae, and that night she was getting set up to
go through all my emails and clean them up, with Jev doing the
categorizing for her too.

I get my emails on my phone and I just kind of ignore them. A friend of
mine emailed me and I guess it got lost in all the other stuff, because I
didn't catch it. Mae told me, hey, this guy, we haven't responded yet.
That was good to know.

The way it works is I have three email accounts, two Gmail and one Zoho,
and Jev goes through all of them and sorts everything into a bunch of
categories like promo and noise and newsletters and personal and finance,
and it does the flagging, and then Mae goes in and deletes only the
categories I designated, the newsletters and the promos and the noise,
without having to ask me anything. Jev is so cheap that having it do the
categorizing saves a lot of tokens. So far nothing has been deleted that
shouldn't have been.

None of them know about each other. If I wanted to know what Claude was
doing I had to ask Claude, and if I wanted to know what the Grok bot was
doing I had to ask the Grok bot. It's like the right hand not knowing what
the left hand is doing. I wasn't copying and pasting between them, though.
They were on separate tasks, each one running its own thing in its own
little universe, and with the screens open I could just read them. It
never really got messy.

I guess you can say I'm the boss and they work for me. I tell them what to
do. Just like normal employees they do some things autonomously, but I'm
the one who set those up. Some people want it to ask permission on
everything and some people don't want to think about it at all. I think
I'm kind of in between.

I just went to a wedding with friends I've had since high school, and most
of them are not real happy about AI right now. I told them where I stood
and what I was using it for, that I'm not doing it to replace me, I'm
doing it to enhance me. They said OK, what I'm using it for seems pretty
good.

Three or four months ago, if you asked me who runs four AIs at once in the
evening, I would have pictured somebody in a dark room with six monitors
up, wearing a hoodie, hacking stuff, not some guy in shorts and a Don't
Panic shirt with a fruit punch who's trying to start a business, with his
personal assistant working on stuff and the coding going and Jev
categorizing for the other two. I kind of wish I was the dark room guy
with the hoodie and six monitors. I just have a laptop and I don't think
it would support six monitors. I don't fit the mold of a hacker. I'm a
vibe coder.

Right now, doing this, my setup is about the same. Claude is on the left
part of my curved monitor. Muse is on the right side, looking through my
past sessions to help jog my memory. The Grok bot is on my laptop screen,
and I asked it what I was doing on September 28th too. When I sat down to
answer questions for this post I had to stop and go figure out what Claude
was even working on that night. I think I'm wearing the same shirt.
