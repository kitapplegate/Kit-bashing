---
title: 'I can see why people are mad at AI'
description: 'I searched for AI on Bluesky looking for people to follow and couldn''t find one positive post.'
pubDate: 'Sep 16 2026'
---

I was lying in bed at night before I went to sleep, scrolling through
social media, which the majority of the people on this planet are doing,
the exact same thing at the exact same time. I was on Bluesky, which is
kind of like X, looking for people to follow. I'm really into AI right
now, and there is a lot to learn, I'll tell you that right now, so I just
typed AI into the search bar.

What came back was memes about how bad AI is, and people saying that
people who use AI are uneducated, and people who use AI are lazy. I don't
remember how long I was scrolling, but I couldn't find a positive post
about it. I mostly use Bluesky with one of my AI agents, which goes out
and researches what people are building so I can get ideas, and there's a
lot of people posting builds on there. There's just not a lot of people
talking positive about AI. I knew the internet was a pretty toxic place,
but I thought it would have at least some positive.

I finally stopped because all I was seeing was the negative stuff, and I
didn't follow anybody. I moved on to scrolling funny memes on Instagram
instead, and I understand the irony of the whole thing, but I was winding
down and that's what I do in bed. I probably shouldn't, but it is what it
is. I hate that phrase anyways.

The uneducated and lazy stuff kind of bristled me a little bit. The way I
like to think of it, AI enhances my abilities, it doesn't replace them.
I've learned so much with AI, I've learned about this tech world and
things within it, and I've learned a lot about myself.

I'll give you an example. My family is pretty spiritual, and I was
thinking about how we could be closer together. We have a group text but
it's just kind of a thing, it's just whatever. So I thought we could have
our own social media platform with a feed, that can also help you write
talks for church and help with your scripture study. I built it with
Claude Code on a Sunday. It took me the day, and the family was using it
the next day.

The talk builder is for brainstorming your talk. You pick a scripture or
type out what you think, and each one goes in its own little thought
bubble, I guess that's the best way I can say it, and you move them around
with your mouse. Before the family ever got it, the bubbles wouldn't move
with the mouse, and it took a couple of different revisions to get it
right.

Six months ago I would have thought, that's kind of cool, I wish I could
do that. Now I had the confidence to say no, I totally can make this, and
I just did it. I wish people looked at it that way, instead of I can't
send emails so I'm gonna have a robot send emails for me. And this is
coming from the guy that's using AI to write a blog. I do it unabashedly
and willingly and knowingly and I'm OK with it.

I can see why people are mad, though. I heard about it on social media and
news articles and I looked it up, and it did happen, especially early on
with building these data centers. There's the [community
water](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2025/07/15/meta-built-a-new-data-center-in-georgia-their-neighbors-water-ran-dry/)
and [electricity prices going
up](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/pjm-data-centers-capacity-auction-imm-bowring/825626/),
and that's a lot of pretty bad things. The companies aren't helping
themselves, that's for sure. They could put them in industrial complexes,
because no one really cares about those areas, obviously, 'cause they're
industrial complexes, instead of right next to a neighborhood. I figure
next to a neighborhood is probably cheaper, and companies tend to go for
the cheaper option.

Then there's the whole book thing, AI companies [buying books and
destroying
them](https://www.techpolicy.press/in-copyright-wins-for-anthropic-and-meta-judges-leave-ample-room-for-future-defeats/)
for training the models. I saw that one on Instagram and looked it up.
That one was Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, which is what I
use. I still like AI and its uses.

I was angry at both, the companies and the people posting, though not
necessarily at the people themselves but at the ignorance of it, and not
looking at what it can do.

The same people who are complaining about AI data centers are loving what
AI is giving them. It's like oil. The people saying Big Oil is bad are
driving cars and using phones, and it takes oil to make phones, and
clothing, and shoes.

I regulated oil companies for 20 years in the Coast Guard, and trust me,
if you're really worried about the environment it's not the big companies.
They get in the news with the bigger spills, but the smaller mom and pop
companies have a lot more spills. In 2006, when I was stationed in Mobile,
Alabama, there was one at a smaller oil terminal, and it had been raining
a lot that week. They're required to have a berm around their storage
tanks to protect the environment in case one spills, and there are valves
that open up so they can get the water out. Somebody left that valve open,
then one of the tanks failed with 3.5 million gallons of oil in it, and it
went into the containment and right out into Mobile Bay. By the time they
discovered it and closed the valve, 400 barrels had gotten out, and a
barrel is 42 gallons. The tank was still leaking about 10 barrels a minute
and we had storms coming in. I was doing contractor oversight on that one,
and we were out there a week with the EPA making sure they cleaned it up.
It didn't even make the local news. That's just one. I've got literally
thousands of them.

Some things that do bad things are still good, kind of like AI. If people
are only seeing the negative and not seeing the benefits, then yes, you
are going to get pushback.

Nothing actually changed that night. I didn't post anything positive and I
didn't try to change the outcome, so maybe I should, but I don't post
anything, negative or positive. I just lurk in the shadows like a creeper.
